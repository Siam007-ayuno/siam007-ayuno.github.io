Pick your style

Three video styles — stick-man, kinetic typography & animated explainer

Order a pack and you do not have to pick just one. Tell me which video gets which style, or write “you choose” and I will match the style to each topic.

Stick-man explainer

A friendly animated host who points at cards, boards and lists while the voice explains. He can stand, point, shrug, wave and think his way through your topic.

Best for: “3 types of…”, steps, how-tos, tiers, plans and comparisons.

Kinetic typography

Giant animated numbers and words popping in sync with the voice — your most surprising figures become the whole show, impossible to scroll past. In this frame the stick-man host introduces the number; in a pure kinetic cut, the type carries the video alone.

Best for: money, stats, prices, percentages and shocking numbers.

Animated explainer

Full animated scenes with icons, bars and simple charts that draw the idea while it is explained — the classic explainer look.

Best for: concepts, products, services and “how it works” videos.

    Questions before you order — FAQ

    Can you make an ad for my product or brand?

    Yes — that is the featured work at the top of this page. I made three finished ads for PAW PAW BD, a real pet store in Dhaka: a Grand Opening brand promo (38 sec), an Omega-3 Fish Oil product ad (42 sec) and a Vet Coach Fish Oil ad for cats (74 sec), all with the client’s real product photos, logo, prices and offers. Send me your product photo, logo, price or offer and your call-to-action (shop link, page or phone number), and I will build the same kind of ad for you — in English or Bangla.

    Can you make a video longer than 30 seconds?

    Yes — that is the point of this page. Pick up to 60 or up to 90 seconds in the price builder, or message me for a long-form explainer (2–10 minutes) in the same styles at a custom quote. My own channel’s demos run 30–46 seconds, and longer cuts follow the same format.

    Can I mix lengths and styles in one order?

    Yes. A pack can mix styles freely — for example two stick-man videos and one kinetic. Mixed lengths are priced per video from the length table; copy a brief from the builder for each length, or just message me the list and I will send one combined quote before you order.

    Can you write the script?

    Yes. Script writing is included in Premium, and a +$10 add-on on Basic and Standard. Send me the topic and the key points — and any facts or numbers that must be exact. You are responsible for the accuracy of your facts; I animate what you give me.

    Do you use my voice or an AI voice?

    Your choice, same price. Send your own voiceover file (MP3 or WAV) and I will build to it, or I will use a clear American-English AI voice like the one in every demo on this page.

    Can you make videos about topics other than money?

    Yes. The styles work for any explainer — business, tech, health, education, products and services. Money is simply what my own channel covers. Send your topic and I will tell you honestly if it fits before you order.

    What format do I receive, and can I use it commercially?

    An MP4 in the format you choose — vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920), square 1:1 or widescreen 16:9 — at 30fps, ready to upload. Format is a free choice, never a paid extra. Commercial use is included in every package.

    How fast will I get my video?

    Standard delivery is 2–5 days for a single video depending on length, 4 days for the 3-pack and 7 days for the 5-pack. Need it sooner? Fast delivery is a +$10 extra on the gig and gets your order to you in 1 day. I will always confirm the delivery date in writing before you order.
